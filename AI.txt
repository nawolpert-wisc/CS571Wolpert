Updates made:
The map now supports click-to-pin: clicking a marker (or a row in the "Key Locations" list) locks its description in the 
detail panel until you click a different marker/row or click elsewhere, instead of the description vanishing the moment you 
moved the mouse off. Markers still show a quick preview on hover, and a browser-default focus outline that appeared on click was 
suppressed in favor of the app's own circle highlight. The list initially got the same hover-preview treatment as the markers, but 
that caused hovering to get stuck — since hovering a row swapped it out for the detail view, the row's own mouse-leave never fired. 
That was resolved by dropping hover from the list entirely, leaving it click-only while the markers keep both hover and click.

Added a Supabase-backed community wildlife-sighting system, submissions insert into a sightings table and appear as dots on the 
island map, matched to a location picked from a fuzzy-search with in the search field. Hovering a sighting dot shows a tooltip with 
species, date, and description, grouping multiple reports at the same spot into one list; visitors can also upvote a sighting as 
"seen again recently," which bumps a confirmation count and timestamp and re-sorts each location's list by most recently confirmed.

I had claude run an accessibility audit and where it fixed the highest-impact findings: paired form labels to their fields via htmlFor/id, 
added proper combobox/listbox ARIA to the location picker and search bar, made map markers and location-list rows 
keyboard-operable (they were mouse-only), gave the icon-only mobile nav toggle an accessible name plus Escape-to-close and 
focus management, rewrote accommodation alt text that didn't match its photo, and corrected two text-contrast values that measured 
below WCAG AA.

I also had claude do some performance/reliability cleanup: lazy-loading on below-the-fold images (with the hero image kept eager/high-priority 
as the page's LCP element), and a visible loading/error state for the sightings fetch instead of it failing silently. By prompting it to 
look at other opportunities for optimization.
